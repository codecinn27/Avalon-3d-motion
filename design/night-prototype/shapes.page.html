Four 3D shapes for the night

The eyes on the cards are already in the plan. These are the extra shapes you could add. Drag any tile to spin it. Then switch them on and off on the table below to see them together.

Narrator's lantern

Its job: the only light of the night, hovering over the middle of the table. Its glow turns red when evil wakes, white when Merlin wakes and gold for Percival, and it lifts away at dawn.

Cost: six glass panes, two caps, three flames. About 30 elements. Phone: keep.

Crystals

Its job: marks who is lit. Red over the traitors, gold over the two cards Percival cannot tell apart. Slow spin and a gentle bob.

Cost: eight facets each, and four crystals are reused from seat to seat. Phone: keep, spin slower.

Moon and drifting stars

Its job: says "it is night" without a word. The moon rises behind the table and sets at dawn. The gold motes sit at different depths, so they slide past each other when the camera orbits.

Cost: one sphere and about 24 dots. Phone: 8 dots.

Spinning focus rings

Its job: shows whose view you are in. When a seat is tapped, three gold rings spin round that card while the camera flies in.

Cost: three bordered circles and a small orb. Phone: keep.
