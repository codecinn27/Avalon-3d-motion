3. How the ten votes appear and turn

Four Approve (white), six Reject (black). The story rule is "everyone votes, all at once, in the open".

ACards at each seat flip together

Each seat lays a card face down in front of it. All ten flip at the same moment. Keeps who voted what.

BPile in the middle, then fan out

Cards slide to the centre, shuffle, and fan out face up. Looks like a real count but hides who voted what.
