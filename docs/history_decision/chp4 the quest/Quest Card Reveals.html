Quest Card Reveals
Avalon · Chapter 4 · The Quest

How should the visitor turn the quest cards over?

The team has laid three quest cards face down and shuffled them. Try each way below. In all four, the last card turned is always the Fail, so the reveal keeps its build up. Scroll on shows the page turning whatever is left, so nobody gets stuck.

A

Tap to flip

Recommended

Tap or click a card. The Fail turns slower and heavier.

Feel
Quick and satisfying. Every flip is a small reward, and the slow Fail lands as the punch.
Phone
Thumb friendly. A tap never fights the page scroll.
Keys
Tab to a card, press Enter.
Build
Small.
B

Press and hold

Hold a card until the gold line fills. Let go early and it settles back.

Feel
Suspense builds while the card trembles. The Fail takes a longer hold.
Phone
Works, but people who just tap see nothing happen, so the hint has to say "Hold".
Keys
Hold Enter or Space.
Build
Medium.
C

Drag to peel

Drag a card upward to lift its back and peek. Lift past halfway and it turns over.

Feel
The most physical, like checking your card at a poker table.
Phone
A drag that starts on a card can't scroll the page. A half peek shows a face early, so the "last card is the Fail" trick can be caught.
Keys
Enter turns it fully (no peeking).
Build
Largest.
D

Watch only

The scroll turns them one by one, like Chapters 2 and 3. Drag the slider, or press Play.

Feel
Cinematic and in step with the chapters before it, but hands off.
Phone
Nothing to learn.
Keys
Nothing to focus.
Build
Smallest. The visitor first touches something in Chapter 5.
