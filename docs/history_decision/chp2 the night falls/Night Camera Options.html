Avalon · Chapter 2 · Night Falls

Three ways to move the camera

All three stages play the same night on the same table. Press Play, or drag the scroll bar like you would scroll the page. Then tap any seat in a stage to see the explorer fly in.

A · Orbit and dolly Recommended

The camera swings round the table so the waking seat comes to the front, leans in to about three times the size, then pulls back out between steps. During a close-up the view tilts up to about 48 degrees, then returns to your 28 degree view.

  • Every painting arrives upright and facing you, whichever seat it is.
  • The strongest sense of moving through a 3D room.
  • The table turns, so seat 1 is not always at the bottom during the night.
  • A little more code than B, about 40 lines.

B · Straight dolly

The camera slides over the table and zooms straight onto each card. The table never turns and the angle never changes.

  • The simplest to build, and the table never moves out of place.
  • Calm. Nothing swings around.
  • Cards on the far side arrive upside down and at a flat angle.
  • Feels more like a zoom than a camera in a room.

C · Zoom only on taps

The story stays on the wide view. Cards glow and stand up where they sit. The camera flies in only when the visitor taps a seat.

  • The calmest story. The zoom becomes a reward for tapping.
  • Least motion, so the lightest on phones.
  • A visitor who only scrolls never sees a painting up close.
  • Gives up the close up on each eye opening that the story line asks for.

What to try. Press Play and watch the Assassin, Morgana and Mordred wake. Then stop on "Explore", tap Percival in each stage, and switch the small Sees and Seen by buttons. Tell me A, B or C. You can also mix: for example A for the story and C's quieter wide view on phones.
